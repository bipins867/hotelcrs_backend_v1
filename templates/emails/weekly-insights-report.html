2. Understanding How Dynamic Pricing Works for You

Action: Email wecare@wchotels.com or call +91 9999880803, 9999880833, 7399888822 to activate Dynamic Rates, which our revenue specialists will configure and manage.

Dynamic pricing uses "demand-based revenue optimization," raising prices when demand is high and adjusting them when demand slows to maintain competitiveness and occupancy.

Factors considered for dynamic pricing:

  • Seasonal trends and local events
  • Competitor pricing from nearby hotels
  • Room type performance
  • Day of the week patterns
  • OTA ranking influence

Dynamic pricing helps properties achieve a balance between occupancy and profitability. Hotels using it see 15-30% more revenue than those using flat rates.

Our Assistance: Our revenue management team will set up pricing matrices, configure daily rate updates, and ensure synchronization with OTAs through "World Choice Hotels," requiring no manual effort from the user.

3. The Power of Promotions – How Special Deals Boost Bookings

Promotions are the fastest way to higher OTA visibility, capable of pushing hotels to the top of search results on platforms like Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, MakeMyTrip, Yatra, EaseMyTrip, Goibibo, Spodia, and Cleartrip.

Benefits of Regular Promotions:

  • Improve OTA ranking and visibility
  • Increase conversion rate by 20-40%
  • Attract new guests during low-demand periods
  • Build brand loyalty through special offers
  • Strengthen repeat bookings and retention

Popular types of promotions include:

  • Early Bird Offers: Rewarding advance bookers with 10-15% off
  • Last-Minute Deals: Aimed to fill unsold rooms close to arrival dates
  • Weekend Offers: Designed to attract leisure travelers
  • Extended Stay Discounts: Intended to encourage longer stays

How We Help: World Choice Hotels can manage these promotions across Online Travel Agencies (OTAs). Email wecare@wchotels.com with preferred dates or discount ranges for campaign implementation and performance monitoring.

4. Last-Minute Rates and Real-Time Adjustments

Last-Minute Rates can attract spontaneous travelers searching within 24-72 hours. OTA traffic patterns and booking windows are analyzed to create time-sensitive discounts, aiming to list properties among top options and increase short-term bookings by 10-25%.
